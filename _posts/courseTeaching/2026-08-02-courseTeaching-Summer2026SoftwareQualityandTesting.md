## Course Overview

This course delves deeply into software quality assurance, examining various testing processes, methodologies, techniques, and tools. Covered topics encompass formal review techniques, black box and white box testing, integration testing, acceptance testing, regression testing, and performance testing. Through hands-on activities, students will acquire the necessary skills to verify the accuracy and quality of developed systems.

## Reflections

- **What worked well:**  
  > The asynchronous course structure, combined with clear weekly modules, allowed students to learn at their own pace while maintaining a consistent progression through software quality and testing concepts.
  > Interactive activities, Bonus Tasks, peer reviews, and practical case studies helped students engage with concepts beyond memorization and apply testing principles to real-world scenarios.
  > The two-phase project approach (Software Test Plan followed by Test Execution and Reporting) effectively connected theoretical concepts with practical software testing practices.

- **What could be improved:**  
  > Provide additional early guidance and examples for the first assignment/project activities to help students adapt faster to the asynchronous learning style and expectations.
  > Improve progress tracking checkpoints throughout the modules to encourage students to stay on pace in the compressed 8-week format.

- **Student feedback highlights:**  
  > Not a single student completed the course reflection survey.
