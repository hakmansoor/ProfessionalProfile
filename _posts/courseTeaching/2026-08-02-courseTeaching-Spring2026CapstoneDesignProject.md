## Course Overview

This course offers you an in-depth exploration of the principles and practices of software engineering. With a strong emphasis on hands-on learning, you will delve into the entire software development lifecycle, mastering essential skills. Topics include methods and tools for software specification, design, and documentation, software development processes, professional ethics, responsibility, and liability in the software lifecycle. You will learn about current software engineering practices and tools, and complete team projects in the process. Interaction with project sponsors from industry, government, and academia will provide realistic experience with software engineering from a professional perspective. As part of the course outcome, you will also hone your abilities in both oral and written communication.

## Reflections

- **What worked well:**  
      Initial onboarding and expectations alignment took longer than anticipated in earlier offerings of the course. To address this, we introduced a “Start Here” orientation featuring interactive slides presented as a conversation between the instructor and an imaginary student. This self-paced experience guides students through course expectations, policies, deliverables, and common questions before the semester begins. The change significantly improved onboarding efficiency, accelerated team formation, reduced repetitive administrative questions, and allowed students to begin project work with a clearer understanding of course expectations.

- **What could be improved:**  
      While the new “Start Here” interactive orientation significantly improved onboarding and reduced questions about course expectations, some students still experienced confusion when documenting progress in the Kansas Board System (KBS), particularly regarding the level of detail expected in tickets versus the Risks, Challenges, and Lessons Learned sections. In the next iteration, the progress reporting structure and accompanying examples will be further refined to provide clearer guidance, improve consistency across teams, and reduce ambiguity in documenting project progress.

- **Student feedback highlights:**  
  > “This was the most realistic preparation for my current job as a junior developer.”  
  > “I appreciated having someone from industry review our work—it felt like what I’d experience on the job.”
