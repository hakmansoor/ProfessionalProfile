## Course Overview

This course introduces students to the concept of Software Quality Assurance (SQA) including principles, component, process, models, standards and certification of SQA.  Students are required to understand the relationship between software quality assurance and software engineering.

---

## Course Snapshot

- **Credits:** [3]
- **Audience:** [Undergraduates]
- **Delivery Mode:** [In-person]
- **No. of Students:** [54]
- **Students Evaluation:** [87%]

---

## Teaching Objectives

1. Inquire a knowledge of main software quality assurance activities, their tasks, work products and their models. 

2. Organize software product quality related activities by applying ISO and IEEE standards.

3. Work in a team and present the team decision/solution for a given tasks.
