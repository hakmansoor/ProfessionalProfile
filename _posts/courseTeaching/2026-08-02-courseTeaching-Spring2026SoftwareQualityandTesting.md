## Course Overview

This course delves deeply into software quality assurance, examining various testing processes, methodologies, techniques, and tools. Covered topics encompass formal review techniques, black box and white box testing, integration testing, acceptance testing, regression testing, and performance testing. Through hands-on activities, students will acquire the necessary skills to verify the accuracy and quality of developed systems.

## Reflections

- **What worked well:**  
      The combination of active learning, real-world case studies, iterative ticket-based work, and frequent peer presentations significantly increased student engagement and helped students connect software testing theory to professional practice.

- **What could be improved:**  
      Introduce the case study representatives and codebase onboarding earlier in the semester, and provide a standardized orientation session to help groups begin their collaboration more efficiently and reduce initial uncertainty.

- **Student feedback highlights:**  
  > “The peer feedback style was always very helpful, receiving feedback from like minded individuals was always nice. We also typically got feedback from the TA adding another layer of help that was beneficial. Additionally, in class questions were always welcome, and the incentivized answering via points was beneficial for participation.”  
  > “I think I benefited greatly from the collaboration posed within this class. I also could concentrate a lot better thanks to the use of the discussions as opposed to speaking to the entire class.”

  > “Assignments combined with peer review were very good. Also, the structure of learn the material then apply was very good.”
