## Course Overview

Basics of user interfaces. Use of requirements gathering techniques. Finding out about user, tasks and environment. Conceptual design. Interaction design. Design principles. Interaction styles. Designing GUI. Designing for web. User interface evaluation process.

## Teaching Objectives

1. To inquire a knowledge of  different design approaches in common use; how cognitive, social, and affective issues apply to interaction design.

2. To apply various techniques to carryout interaction design. In particular, how to identify users’ needs and the context of their activities.

3. To work in a team and present the team decision/solution for a given tasks.
