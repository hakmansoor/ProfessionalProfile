## 🧪 Mentored Undergraduate Research Program
Since 2024, I have developed a structured 12-week program for undergraduates to engage in research:
- **Weeks 1–6**: Literature exploration and analysis of 5–10 relevant papers  
- **Weeks 7–12**: Structured breakdown using a custom research framework including identifying research questions, variables, tools, and experimental designs  
- Weekly discussions are held to deepen understanding and develop research skills.
