## 🎤 Guest Speaker – YATAKA LEGEND SDN BHD Workshops (2011)

I was honored to be invited as a **Speaker** in a series of professional training workshops organized by **YATAKA LEGEND SDN BHD, Malaysia**.  
These workshops were part of YATAKA’s academic and professional development initiatives, engaging participants in practical and strategic areas of management and leadership with visiting international guests.  

---

## Sessions Delivered  

- 📅 **May 16th – May 18th, 2011**  
  🏛️ *Location: Malaysia*  
  **Topic:** *Strategic Planning*  
  - Focused on methods for setting organizational goals, aligning resources, and ensuring long-term sustainability.  

- 📅 **July 04th – July 07th, 2011**  
  🏛️ *Location: Malaysia*  
  **Topic:** *Project Management*  
  - Delivered insights into planning, execution, and control of projects with emphasis on effective leadership.  

- 📅 **August 09th – August 11th, 2011**  
  🏛️ *Location: Malaysia*  
  **Topic:** *Creative Thinking*  
  - Engaged participants in innovative problem-solving and idea-generation techniques for organizational growth.  

---

## Reflection  

The **2011 series of workshops** provided me with a unique opportunity to engage with professionals across diverse industries in Malaysia.  
By covering topics from **strategic planning** to **project management** and **creative thinking**, the sessions emphasized both the structured and innovative sides of leadership.  

These engagements not only strengthened my ability to deliver knowledge across multidisciplinary contexts but also highlighted the importance of **adaptability, bilingual communication, and cross-cultural engagement** in professional development.
