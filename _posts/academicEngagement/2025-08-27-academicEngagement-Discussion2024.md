## 📖 Collaborative Book Project: Software Engineering & AI Collaboration (2024)

I am pleased to have received an invitation to participate in a **collaborative book writing project** entitled:

**Software Engineering & AI Collaboration**  

This initiative aims to **adapt findings from the Agenda for Software Engineering Research & Development**, envisioning a shift in the traditional software development pipeline toward a model where **AI and humans collaborate** to continuously evolve systems based on software engineer intent.

### Purpose
The book addresses core challenges in the **development and maintenance of software systems**, preparing readers to adapt to **evolving technological advancements and market shifts**. It emphasizes:

- Integrating AI into the software development lifecycle  
- Enhancing collaboration between human engineers and AI systems  
- Preparing professionals for future multidisciplinary research and industry challenges  

### Participation
I have been invited to contribute to **research discussions from 26th May to 30th May 2024**. This collaboration provides opportunities for:

- Knowledge sharing and scholarly exchange  
- Innovation in software engineering practices  
- Advancing research at the intersection of AI and software engineering  

This opportunity represents a meaningful engagement in shaping **next-generation software engineering methodologies** in a global, collaborative context.
