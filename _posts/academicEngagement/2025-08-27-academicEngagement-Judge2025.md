## 🖥️ Software Engineering Projects Day – University of Oklahoma (2025)

I was honored to be invited as a **Faculty Judge** for the **Software Engineering Projects Day**, held on **Tuesday, May 6th, 2025**, at **The University of Oklahoma**.  

During the event, I evaluated student projects and provided feedback on their software engineering work. Additionally, the event provided an opportunity to explore **collaboration initiatives** between the **University of Oklahoma School of Computer Science** and the **University of Maryland, Baltimore County Software Engineering MPS program**.  

This engagement highlights my continued involvement in fostering student learning and academic collaboration across institutions.
