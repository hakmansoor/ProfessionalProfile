## 🎓 Annual Postgraduate Colloquium – University Malaysia Pahang (2019)

I was pleased to receive an invitation to participate in the **Annual Postgraduate Colloquium** held from **20th May to 31st May 2019** at the **Software Engineering Lab, Faculty of Computer Systems & Software Engineering, University Malaysia Pahang (UMP), Kuantan, Pahang**.  

The colloquium included:

- Supervisors meetings  
- Postgraduate student progress presentations and reports  
- Research workshops  
- Research brainstorming sessions  

As a **leader of Research Grant (No. 170102)** and faculty member at the **University of Prince Mugrin (UPM), Madinah, Saudi Arabia**, I contributed to discussions aimed at **promoting academic and scholarly exchanges** and **advancing research in multi-disciplinary fields**.  

This collaboration focused not only on **knowledge sharing** but also on **fostering innovation**. Accommodation was provided during the invitation period, facilitating full engagement in the colloquium activities.
