## 👥 The Challenges of Managing People Workshop – Jakarta, Indonesia (2015)

I was invited as a **Guest Speaker and Moderator** for the **“The Challenges of Managing People”** workshop held from **June 1st to June 10th, 2015** at the **Millennium Hotel Sirih, Jakarta, Indonesia**.  

Organized by **Yataka Legend Sdn Bhd**, the workshop focused on effective **people management strategies**, team leadership, and organizational behavior. My role involved delivering bilingual sessions in **Arabic and English** and engaging participants in discussions on practical management challenges.
