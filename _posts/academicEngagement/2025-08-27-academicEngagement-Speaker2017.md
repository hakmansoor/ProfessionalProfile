## 🌍 E-Government Project Workshop – Khartoum, Sudan (2017)

I was honored to be invited as a **Speaker** at the **E-Government Project Workshop**, organized jointly by **YATAKA LEGEND SDN BHD (Malaysia)** and **SUDAPET (Sudan)**. The workshop took place from **October 1st to October 5th, 2017** in **Khartoum, Sudan**.  

As a bilingual speaker in **Arabic and English**, I shared my expertise in **Project Management**, contributing to the knowledge and professional development of the participants.  

---

## Reflection  
This workshop was an excellent opportunity to contribute to the development of E-Government initiatives in Sudan. Engaging with participants from diverse professional backgrounds highlighted the critical role of project management in implementing large-scale digital transformation projects.

Delivering the session in both Arabic and English allowed me to bridge communication gaps and ensure accessibility, which reinforced the importance of adaptability and cultural awareness in international collaborations.

The experience not only enhanced my cross-cultural communication skills but also deepened my appreciation for the challenges and opportunities faced by governments in adopting modern technologies for public service improvement.
