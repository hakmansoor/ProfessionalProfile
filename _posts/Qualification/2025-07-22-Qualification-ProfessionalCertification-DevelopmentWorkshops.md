## 🎓 Certifications & Workshops

A collection of professional development programs completed at **Universiti Malaysia Pahang** between 2015 and 2016:

- **NEW ACADEMIA LEARNING INNOVATIONS** (2016)
- **WRITING AND ASSESSMENT FOR LOCAL GRANTS** (2016)
- **GET-IT SEMINAR** (2016)
- **STRATEGIC 2016 FSKKP DEVELOPMENT PLAN** (2016)
- **WORKSHOP ON ICGPA** (2016)
- **EFFECTIVE TEACHING AND LEARNING: UNIT 1 (TEACHING IN LARGE AND SMALL GROUPS)** (2015)

These engagements supported continuous learning, grant development capacity, curriculum enhancement, and innovation in teaching strategies.
