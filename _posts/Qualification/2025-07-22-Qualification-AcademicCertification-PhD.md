## 📄 Dissertation  
**Title:** *An Ontology-Based Approach for Test Case Management System Using Semantic Technology*  

This research proposed a semantic-driven approach to enhance test case management by utilizing ontology-based models. The framework improves reusability, traceability, and intelligent test case retrieval in software testing workflows.
